Docking: a tab dropped on a tab row lands among its tabs (in front of a tab, or after it from its trailing half), even at the workspace's wall, with a bar showing where (`panelsActions.moveTab`, `moveTab` in core); one dropped in the middle of its own group's body floats. A floating group takes no drops, so the workspace under it does. `CodeEditor` takes `wrap`, to wrap long lines. In a `PropertyGrid`, a switch or checkbox sits at its row's far end.
