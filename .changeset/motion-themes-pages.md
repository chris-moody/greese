**The motion themes change pages too (ADR 0029).** Snappy pops pages away and in; Gentle fades the old page while the new one comes into focus; Wipe wipes the next page over the last, from the right going forward and the left going back; Pocket sinks pages into a slot at their foot and raises the next out of it (and the reverse going back); Depth turns pages like the faces of a cube. Each theme's page duration, ease, stagger (and Snappy's exit order) match its story.
