EditableField's `clearable` puts an × inside text, number and the new `textarea` fields that empties them, and gives a select a first option for its own cleared value (`clearValue`, `clearLabel`). NumberField takes `clearable` too. ScrollCarousel's `strip` makes it a row of controls that scrolls when it overflows: slides as wide as their content, no carousel semantics of its own, pointer-only buttons that page along.
