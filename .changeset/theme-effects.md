Effects can be retuned and added by a theme. The kit's `keyframes()` mixin replaces an entrance preset's `@keyframes` (or adds one), `defineGreeseEffect()` under a built-in's name now replaces that effect in the runtime, and `removeGreeseEffect()` forgets a definition.
