Add `@greese/svg`: type-safe SVG drawing and animation with no dependencies. Build paths and shapes, convert between them, measure them without a DOM, morph any shape into another (points and parts matched and aligned), draw, fill, drain and offset stroke slices by percent or run them as marquees, move elements along paths, and build gradients, filters (with `glow`, `goo`, `grain` and `distort` presets), clip paths and masks. Animations are properties on small controls that any tweener drives, greese.js included, or the built-in `tween()`. `@greese/react/svg` adds `Drawing`, `Path`, `DrawPath`, `MarqueePath`, `Morph`, `LinearGradient`, `RadialGradient`, `Filter`, `ClipPath` and `Mask`, and re-exports `@greese/svg`.
