Add `@greese/authoring`, editor UI for greese (ADR 0035): `PanelDock`, a dockable workspace of tab groups around a stage and timeline, where panels drag to any edge, join groups as tabs, dock along the workspace's edges or float; its layout as data, `LayoutSpec` and `buildFromSpec`, with `useLayoutCache` to remember each workspace; and a Redux store per editor from `createAuthoringEnvironment`, so editors can share a page or nest, with the framework's slices (layers, library, timeline selection and clipboard, navigation, guides, playback and its bounds, time modes, time signatures, rate curves, recording), their selectors, and undo and redo that restore the document while keeping the view (`undoable` marks an edit as a step, `viewSlices` names the slices undo keeps; `useHistory`, and `useUndoRedoKeymap`, which can listen on an editor's root and leaves a text field its own undo). `@greese/authoring/core` adds the layer, frame, tween, library and timing types, the timing model, tick maths, animation and musical time resolutions, and tree, layer and viewport helpers. `HierarchicalList` (with `HierarchicalListItem` and `HierarchicalListFolder`) is a WAI-ARIA tree to select, rename, reorder and nest items by pointer, drag or keyboard, with an actions bar, `LayerTree` (with `LayerTreeItem`) binds it to the editor's layers, with visibility, lock and mute toggles and the config's structural layer types, the stage (`StageHost` mounting a media's backend from `MediaConfig`, `StageContext`, `StageRulers`, `RulerToggle`, `AuthoringBreadcrumbs`) with its driver and subview navigation, `LibraryPanel` (with `LibraryAssetItem` and `SymbolCreateDialog`) keeps the editor's reusable symbols, the stage's controls (`StageBar`, `ZoomControl`, `GridControl`, `StageColorControl`, `LayoutBar`) with `EditableSelect`, a typed value with presets, the stage's guides (`GuidesOverlay`, each guide a keyboard slider, with `GuidePropertiesDialog`), the editor's tools as data (`ToolDefinition`, with `buildToolHotkeys` and `toolForKey` in core) drawn by `Toolbar` (with `ToolbarItem`), a WAI-ARIA toolbar whose groups open a flyout, picked by `useToolHotkeys` and shown on the stage by `createToolCursors`, with `ToolOptionsPanel` for the active tool's settings, the nav bar's parts (`NavBar`, `NavHistory`, `EnvironmentSelect`, `AccentIcon`, and an icon and hue per step of `AuthoringBreadcrumbs`), property fields (`PropertyGrid`, which lines up `EditableField` rows, `ComplexColorField` for a solid, gradient or no fill, `GradientEditor` and `GradientStopBar`, whose stops are keyboard sliders, and `ToggleSelect`) with the paint and gradient maths in core, `CodeEditor` (with `Highlighted`), a code box with highlighting and no dependency that lights a span (`highlight`) and keeps its selection in step with a canvas (`selection`, `onSelectionChange`), on `highlightCode` in core, which reads CSS, SCSS, JavaScript, TypeScript and XML, with its palette as `@greese/authoring/scss/code`, the timeline (`Timeline`, `TimelineHeader`, `TimelinePanel`: layers' frames, keyframes, tweens, spans and markers, the ruler, playhead and playback bounds, the rate, time-signature and marker tools, `TimelineLevelEditor` and `SpanActivityChart`), played by greese.js (`usePlaybackClock`) with tweens eased by greese.js's `cubicBezier`, with `TweenPanel`, `MarkerPanel`, a layer row's `LayerContextMenu` (opened where `contextMenuPoint`, in core, says: it cancels the browser's own menu and gives the pointer's position, or the trigger's corner from the keyboard), and `AuthoringView`, a whole editor around them, `NodeGraph`, a controlled graph of nodes, input ports and edges laid out in columns by depth from its sources (`nodeGraphColumns`), connected from each port's menu or by dragging from an output, with arrow-key focus, selection and Delete to remove (ADR 0039), and search providers (fuzzy, prefix, exact) rank what a filter box finds, indexed once per collection by `useSearchProvider`. Tabs follow the WAI-ARIA tab pattern and splits are keyboard-resizable window splitters. `@greese/authoring/core` and `@greese/authoring/state` run without React; `@reduxjs/toolkit`, `react-redux` and `immer` are peer dependencies.
