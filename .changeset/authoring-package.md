Add `@greese/authoring`, editor UI for greese (ADR 0035): `PanelDock`, a dockable workspace of tab groups around a stage and timeline, where panels drag to any edge, join groups as tabs, dock along the workspace's edges or float; its layout as data, `LayoutSpec` and `buildFromSpec`, with `useLayoutCache` to remember each workspace; and a Redux store per editor from `createAuthoringEnvironment`, so editors can share a page or nest, with the framework's slices (layers, library, timeline selection and clipboard, navigation, guides, playback and its bounds, time modes, time signatures, rate curves, recording), their selectors, and undo and redo that restore the document while keeping the view (`useHistory`, `useUndoRedoKeymap`). `@greese/authoring/core` adds the layer, frame, tween, library and timing types, the timing model, tick maths, animation and musical time resolutions, and tree, layer and viewport helpers. Tabs follow the WAI-ARIA tab pattern and splits are keyboard-resizable window splitters. `@greese/authoring/core` and `@greese/authoring/state` run without React; `@reduxjs/toolkit`, `react-redux` and `immer` are peer dependencies.
