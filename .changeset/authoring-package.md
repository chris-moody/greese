Add `@greese/authoring`, editor UI for greese (ADR 0035): `PanelDock`, a dockable workspace of tab groups around a stage and timeline, where panels drag to any edge, join groups as tabs, dock along the workspace's edges or float; its layout as data, `LayoutSpec` and `buildFromSpec`, with `useLayoutCache` to remember each workspace; and a Redux store per editor from `createAuthoringEnvironment`, so editors can share a page or nest, with the framework's slices (layers, library, timeline selection and clipboard, navigation, guides, playback and its bounds, time modes, time signatures, rate curves, recording), their selectors, and undo and redo that restore the document while keeping the view (`useHistory`, `useUndoRedoKeymap`). `@greese/authoring/core` adds the layer, frame, tween, library and timing types, the timing model, tick maths, animation and musical time resolutions, and tree, layer and viewport helpers. `HierarchicalList` (with `HierarchicalListItem` and `HierarchicalListFolder`) is a WAI-ARIA tree to select, rename, reorder and nest items by pointer, drag or keyboard, with an actions bar, `LayerTree` (with `LayerTreeItem`) binds it to the editor's layers, with visibility, lock and mute toggles and the config's structural layer types, the stage (`StageHost` mounting a media's backend from `MediaConfig`, `StageContext`, `StageRulers`, `RulerToggle`, `AuthoringBreadcrumbs`) with its driver and subview navigation, `LibraryPanel` (with `LibraryAssetItem` and `SymbolCreateDialog`) keeps the editor's reusable symbols, the stage's controls (`StageBar`, `ZoomControl`, `GridControl`, `StageColorControl`, `LayoutBar`) with `EditableSelect`, a typed value with presets, and search providers (fuzzy, prefix, exact) rank what a filter box finds. Tabs follow the WAI-ARIA tab pattern and splits are keyboard-resizable window splitters. `@greese/authoring/core` and `@greese/authoring/state` run without React; `@reduxjs/toolkit`, `react-redux` and `immer` are peer dependencies.
