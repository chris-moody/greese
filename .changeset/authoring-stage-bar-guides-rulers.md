The stage bar's controls are all `xs`: RulerToggle was `sm`. StageContext adds a `GuidesToggle` beside the rulers' (the guides' `hidden` state, `guidesActions.setGuidesHidden`, `selectGuidesHidden`; hidden guides are kept, and a new one shows them again). StageRulers takes `ticks`, a spacing or `'auto'` (`rulerTicks(scale)`), for drawings measured in a few units. `distinguishingColor(index)` and `DISTINGUISHING_COLORS`: Pixels' thirty node colours. GridControl's toggle, the layer rows' toggles, gradient stops, panel collapse and the library's open button get tooltips.
