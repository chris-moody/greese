Brand tones: every `tone` prop takes `primary`, `secondary` and `tertiary` (Button, IconButton, ToggleButton, Badge, Chip, Text, Heading, Link, Alert, Toast, Status, ChatBubble, Step, MenuItem, Stat), typed as the exported `BrandTone`. Chip gains a `tone` prop: a plain chip is tinted in it and a selectable one shows it when selected; without one, chips look as before. A pressed ToggleButton now shows its tone (the default `neutral` still presses to accent).
